# Credit-Card-Retention-Project

## Project Objective
In this project, I used PowerBI to analyze and credit card attrition and usage across a customer base. With this knowledge, I was able to explore what drives customer engagement, churn, and profitability.

## Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/thowardIV/Credit-Card-Retention-Project/blob/main/Credit%20(1).csv">Dataset</a>

## Questions and KPI's
- Which customer attributes are linked to retention or attrition?
- What behavior flags (e.g., inactivity, contact volume) suggest churn?
- How do age, tenure, card type, income, and usage relate to loyalty?
- Which segments are underperforming or at risk?
- What operational actions can improve customer retention?
- Customer Retention Rate
- Attrition Rate
- Average Credit Limit
- Average Utilization Ratio
- Average Transaction Count (Last 12 Months)

## Process
- Verified data for missing values, incorrect data types, and renamed columns.
- Created DAX measures to use in the dashboard.
- Created visuals according to the KPI's wanted.

## Project Insights
- Out of the 10,000 customers, 7,100 of them have been inactive for 2-3 months over the past year.
- The average utilization ratio is 27%, thus the card is not being used as frequently as other cards in their wallets.
- A majority of customers are blue card holders, therefore attrition among blue card holders impact overall profitability.
- Many customers fall in the less than $40k income category and the 30-49 age group.

## Recommendations

Some recommendations to reduce attrition are implementing a re-engagement campaign, creating incentives to increase utilization, and optimizing customer contact logic. The bank should contact customers once they hit 45 days of inactivity. Don’t let the customers reach the 2–3-month mark that we see in my dashboard. Some incentives to increase card utilization could be partnering with subscription services to offer credits for setting up recurring autopayments or offering cash back multipliers on groceries or gas. To optimize customer contact, train services reps to identify low utilization accounts and proactively offer tailored products or fee waivers.
